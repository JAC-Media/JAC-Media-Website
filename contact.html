FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Pay-Per-Lead Pilot program work?

We handle 100% of your campaign builds, ad creative creation, and targeting. You only pay a flat $75 per qualified lead we generate during the 30-day window. You maintain total control over your ad spend.

What counts as a “qualified lead”?

We build strict custom lead-filtering forms. A qualified lead means a homeowner in your service area who has explicitly requested an estimate for your service and provided verified contact info.

Do I really get to keep the assets if we part ways?

Yes. If you decide not to transition to a growth tier after 30 days, all the ad accounts, custom forms, and ad graphics remain yours to use forever.

Why do you require a testimonial?

As a performance-driven agency, your localized data and feedback help us refine regional ad strategies. We trade our premium management services for your honest video or written review.
